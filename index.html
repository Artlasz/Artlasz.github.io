Tanya

This was my first time with a project of this scale, a full length animation with a highly detailed character! I think I did pretty well.

This project followed the workflow of blockout, sculpt, retopology, polish, texturing, shape key creation, rigging, weight painting and finally posing!


Making the blockout is simple enough, as it comprises of using a reference to mold simple shapes into the general shape of whatever you want to model. After this came sculpting - which I was already familiar with due to previous projects. During the sculpting phase you turn the blockout from a general shape into the desired shape, refining it and adding a great deal of detail. Sculpting is usually a lengthy procedure, but it gives great control to the modedeller to make fine details.
Then came the retopology, which is done to reduce the amount of vertexes on the model to allow for animation, but without sacrificing much detail. This has to be done due to sculpts having an incredibly high amount of vertexes, which is incompatible with animation. It consists of drawing vertexes over the sculpt for hours on end until the entire model is drawn over - after which you can then rig and weightpaint the model to make it possible to animate. Retopology also allows you to easily edit the model without having to use sculpting tools, this is because you keep the detail but have far fewer vertexes - which translates to more control over the model. The issue was that I had never done retopology on such a scale before and it took a very long time to accomplish with good quality in mind, but the end result was fantastic, with good edge flow and topology.
With retopology done, I modelled the clothing for the character and did a final pass of polish on the full model. This I followed up by making the textures for the model inside of Blender.
Once textured, I was free to start making shape keyes! I made one for blinking. Using shape keyes for animations like this makes them quite smooth, due to shape keyes actually editing the topology of the model.
Finally came the great task of rigging and weight painting the model, so that it could be animated and posed! This was accomplished by making a rig by hand, with inverse kinematics on the legs for natural movement and the hair having its own rig to simulate it moving around with the character. I even went as far to add a custom control rig (which one of the images showcases)!
It was a difficult project, but very worth it with how much I learned from it. I am proud of the results!

The biggest challenge to overcome was definitely rigging and weight painting, which I lacked previous experience with.


Project Info:
I made this full character in around 2 months by myself; I am now able to achieve these kinds of results far faster due to the experience gained.
The tool used was Blender.

Skies of Aether

Skies of Aether is an endless sidescroller with a bullet hell twist! It centers around fighting waves of enemies that get more numerous as time goes on, the goal being to get the highest score.


The greatest challenge in this game was the endlessly looping background - but after much trial and error and working together with my graphics designer, we got it working!


Project Info:
We made the game in 4 days with a team of 3 for a local Game Jam - I functioned as the programmer. Sidenote, we were 2nd place with our game!
The engine used was Unity.



Case of The Forgotten

Case of The Forgotten is a point and click detective game where your goal is to retrieve lost memories in order to solve a strange case - but our detective stumbles into a far deeper hole than he anticipated.


For this game, I learned how to make point and click interactions work in 2d as well as utilising cutscenes to tell a story.


Project Info:
We made the game in 1 week with a team of 4 for a local Game Jam - I functioned as the programmer.
The engine used was Unity.

Gnoming Around

Gnoming Around tells the tale of a garden gnome brought to life by the power of its owner's struggle, it's a 2d platformer with the goal being to finish levels while collecting blueberries.


This was the first game of this type I made, which lead to me learning a wealth of information regarding 2d development.


Project Info:
